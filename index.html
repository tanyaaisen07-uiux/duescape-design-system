Duescape — Design System

Design system extracted from the Duescape mobile app mockups — a subscription & budget tracker. Two themes, one token set. Color values are approximated from screenshots and should be verified against the source file.

01 — Principles

Design Principles

Three rules that make this system Duescape — not just any fintech. Every component below exists to serve one of them.

Clarity First

The most important information is visible without interaction.

In the system: the gradient Balance Card opens every screen — total, active count and next payment in one glance, zero taps.
Action Over Analysis

Recommendations should suggest what to do, not only what happened.

In the system: Subscription Actions tiles, Smart Suggestion with a concrete saving amount, Pause / Cancel / Save changes controls.
Familiar by Default

Interactions follow native mobile patterns.

In the system: iOS-style toggle, bottom tab bar, chevron settings rows, horizontally scrolling category icons.

Palette

06 — Iconography

Icons

Line icons, single stroke weight — matching the mockups. Recommended set: Lucide / Feather (web) or SF Symbols Regular (iOS). Icons inherit text color via currentColor, so they adapt to both themes automatically.

Stroke & sizeStroke 1.5–2px, rounded caps and joins. Three sizes: 16 (inline), 20 (rows), 24 (navigation, tiles).
ContainersCategory icons sit in 44px tiles (r-12) on solid brand colors, white glyph. Settings icons sit in 30px tiles (r-8) on soft semantic fills.
ColorDefault icons use text-1 / text-3; the active nav tab uses blue-600. Never mix filled and outlined styles in one surface.

1

Monthly Total Card

The Monthly Total card is the primary entry point to spending awareness. It answers "how much am I spending?" in under a second: total → active count → next payment, with zero taps. The gradient and card-chip visuals borrow the language of a physical bank card, so the number reads as money, not as a statistic.

Clarity First
2

Subscription Actions

Three tiles sit directly under the total — the answer to the number above them. The dashboard never shows a problem without an exit: add, import, or manage reminders is always one tap away. Insight and action live on the same screen.

Action Over Analysis
3

Bottom Tab Bar

A standard five-tab bottom navigation with the active tab in brand blue. No custom navigation metaphors where a native pattern exists — users already know how to move around before they open the app for the first time.
